# Schedule Refresh Jobs

Confirm the existing artifact, source availability, time zone, cadence, failure behavior, and owner before scheduling. Avoid enabling recurring work when the inputs are unreliable or the recipient has not authorized the schedule. Record the schedule, data window, and failure-notification path; confirm the job after creation.
