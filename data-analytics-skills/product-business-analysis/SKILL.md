# Product And Business Analysis

Frame the decision and comparison before computing. Use traceable sources, show the method and important assumptions, and distinguish findings from interpretation. Quantify uncertainty and alternative explanations. End with a practical recommendation, expected trade-offs, and the next evidence that would change the decision.
