# Convert To Slides

Turn an existing artifact into a decision-oriented narrative: one point per slide, evidence-rich visuals, readable labels, and speaker-friendly implications. Preserve provenance and key caveats. Inspect the rendered deck for clipping, contrast, hierarchy, and chart legibility before delivery.
