# Market Sizing

Define the market boundary, geography, period, buyer, and unit before estimating. Choose top-down, bottom-up, or triangulated methods suited to available evidence. Expose assumptions and calculation steps, use scenario ranges, test sensitivities, and state which data would most improve confidence. Never present an assumption as observed market fact.
