# Gather Business Context

Collect only enough context to make later analysis meaningful: the decision, why it matters now, the metric or entity definition, time period, owners, known changes, and source-of-truth artifacts. Separate verified facts from open questions. When the request already supplies this framing, skip collection and proceed to the focused analysis.
